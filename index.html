About

Hardware that works on the first try.

I'm an NPI technical lead with five years of engineering experience, more than four of them in Final Test at NXP Semiconductors. I've owned over 50 new product introductions from start to finish — each one delivered first time right and on time, with no design re-spins and a 20% shorter cycle.

I bring together project leadership and hands-on engineering: schedule, budget, suppliers and global teams on one side; test hardware, PCB design, DOE and simulation on the other. That combination helped secure about US$40M in revenue through a single platform conversion.
